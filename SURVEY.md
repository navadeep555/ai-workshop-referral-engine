# Student Survey: AI Projects and Placements
Build it in **Google Forms** (forms.google.com). Keep it under 2 minutes, because short surveys get more replies.
In Form settings, turn **off** "Collect email addresses" so students answer honestly and you don't hold anyone's personal data.

---

## Form title
**Quick 1-minute survey: AI projects & placements (final-year students)**

## Form description
Hi! I'm a final-year student at Amrita researching how students like us prepare for placements with AI. This is anonymous and takes about a minute. Thank you! 🙏

---

## Questions

**1. Which year are you in?** *(multiple choice, required)*
- Final year
- Pre-final year
- Other

**2. Your branch** *(multiple choice, required)*
- CSE / CSE (AI/ML) / CSE (DS)
- IT
- ECE / EEE
- Mechanical / Civil / Other

**3. Do you have an AI project on your resume right now?** *(multiple choice, required)*
- Yes, one I built and can explain
- Yes, but I mostly copied or followed a tutorial
- No

**4. Has anyone asked you about AI in a placement interview or test?** *(multiple choice)*
- Yes
- No
- Haven't had interviews yet

**5. What has stopped you from building an AI project?** *(checkboxes, pick up to 2)*
- Don't know where to start
- No time with classes and placement prep
- Courses are too long or paid
- I think it needs strong maths or ML
- Nothing stopped me, I've built one
- Other: ___

**6. You see these three invites in your class WhatsApp group. Which one would you actually click?** *(multiple choice, required)*
- A) "Placement interviews now ask about AI. Learn how to answer in 60 minutes, for free."
- B) "Build and deploy your first AI project in 60 minutes. Get a live link and GitHub repo for your resume tonight."
- C) "40 students from our college already joined a free AI workshop. Join them."

**7. When could you attend a free 60-minute live online workshop?** *(multiple choice)*
- Weekday evening (7–9 PM)
- Saturday or Sunday morning
- Saturday or Sunday evening
- I wouldn't attend

**8. Where do you actually notice event and workshop announcements?** *(checkboxes, pick up to 2)*
- Class or batch WhatsApp group
- College club announcements
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- Email
- Friends telling me directly

**9. If a friend sent you a personal invite link to a free workshop, would you be more likely to join?** *(linear scale 1–5)*
1 = No difference → 5 = Much more likely

**10. (Optional) In one line, what would make a free workshop worth an hour of your evening?** *(short answer)*

> 👉 [form link]

**Where to send it:** your batch group, your section group, any club groups you're in, and 3–5 friends in other branches or colleges (ask them to forward it). Post once in the morning and send one gentle reminder in the evening. **Aim for 60 or more responses.**

## What each question proves in your plan

| Question | Claim in your plan it backs up (or disproves) |
|---|---|
| Q3 | "Most students don't have a real AI project" → your **"why they'd care"** |
| Q4 | "Interviews are asking about AI" → your **placement hook** |
| Q5 | The real barrier → your **message** (e.g., if "don't know where to start" wins, lead with "no AI experience needed") |
| Q6 | **A/B/C test of your hook:** this is your strongest slide, and it tells you which invite text to use |
| Q7 | The **workshop timing** to choose |
| Q8 | Real **channel ranking**: does WhatsApp actually beat Instagram or LinkedIn? |
| Q9 | Whether your **referral loop** is worth building |
| Q10 | Real quotes to use in your slides and video |

**Important:** if the data disagrees with the plan, change the plan. That makes a perfect answer to *"What did AI suggest that you rejected, and why?"* For example: "AI assumed LinkedIn would work. My survey showed only 9% notice events there, so I dropped it."

When responses are in, open the **Responses** tab, click the Sheets icon, download it as CSV and send it to me. I'll turn it into charts and update the plan with the real numbers.
